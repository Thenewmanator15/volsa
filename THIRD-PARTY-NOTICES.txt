THIRD-PARTY NOTICES FOR VOXA
============================

Voxa is built from the 206 packages below, other people's work, each under
its own licence. For each, this file gives its licence and repository and the
licence and notice texts that come with it. Where a package offers a choice of
licences, Voxa takes the most permissive of those it accepts, which "Voxa takes
it under" names, and the texts of the others are left out. For a package that
comes with no text of its licence, the standard text stands in, with the
copyright lines of the licence file in its repository where that file has any.
Texts that are the same word for word are written once, under TEXTS, and
referred to by number.

Voxa's own licence is in LICENSE-MIT and LICENSE-APACHE, and its own notice,
with the credit to SynthSeg, is in NOTICE.

`cargo xtask notices` writes this file from the packages compiled into the
Voxa program for 64-bit Windows. Do not edit it by hand.

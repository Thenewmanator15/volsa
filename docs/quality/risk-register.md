# Risk register

What could lead someone to be misled or harmed by using Voxa, what keeps that from
happening, and how that is checked. Kept in the spirit of ISO 14971. Voxa is not a medical
device and is not for diagnostic use; the register exists because people will still look
at their own scans with it and draw conclusions.

How bad (severity): **minor** (confusion, wasted time), **serious** (a person believes
something false about their own or someone's body, and may worry, delay or act on it),
**critical** (care is changed because of Voxa). How likely, before the control:
**rare**, **possible**, **likely**. Each entry is reviewed whenever the part of Voxa it names
changes.

Entries from earlier work (labels on the wrong grid, left and right, a failed model check
hidden) are brought in when that work is next changed.

| R-UI-01 | A plain name calls a scan what it is not (a T2 called T1, a spine called brain); the person compares or measures the wrong scan and believes a difference that is not there. | serious / possible | Names use only the scanner's own words and the file's facts, never timings (UI-11); the original description is always under the name (UI-10); unknown words give a facts-only name. | Tests of the naming on made-up descriptions, including ones meant to mislead (`crates/voxa-app/src/naming/tests.rs::conflicting_weightings_name_none`, `crates/voxa-app/src/naming/tests.rs::words_are_matched_whole_without_regard_to_case_or_punctuation`, `crates/voxa-app/src/naming/tests.rs::timings_never_name_a_weighting` and `crates/voxa-app/src/naming/modality_tests.rs::nothing_the_scanner_wrote_outside_the_vocabulary_changes_the_name`). | serious / rare |
| R-UI-02 | A value is shown in HU when the file's values are not HU (a derived or secondary CT), or a CT window is applied to values in other units; the person reads a density that is false. | serious / possible | HU only for CT whose rescale says HU or nothing (UI-19); other values say "no unit"; the profile is from Modality only (UI-17); a CT window is greyed, with why, while the scan's values are not in HU (UI-18). | Tests with made-up CT files with and without rescale types (`crates/voxa-app/src/profile/tests.rs::hu_only_for_ct_whose_rescale_says_hu_or_nothing`, `crates/voxa-app/src/ui/viewport/tests.rs::a_non_hu_ct_value_has_no_unit`, `crates/voxa-app/src/ui/planes/tests.rs::the_value_under_the_pointer_on_a_plane_is_read_from_the_volume_in_the_scans_unit`, `crates/voxa-app/src/ui/commands/table_tests.rs::a_ct_window_is_greyed_with_a_reason_for_a_ct_whose_values_are_not_in_hu` and `crates/voxa-app/src/ui/tabs/view/tests.rs::the_fine_tune_fields_say_hu_when_the_values_are_in_hounsfield_units`). | serious / rare |
| R-UI-03 | A length is given in millimetres when the file's scale is absent (the index fills in 1 mm today, harmless only while measuring needs a full volume) or is a detector's (an X-ray's magnification), so a size is wrong. | serious / possible | The index records whether the spacing was given; no mm without Pixel Spacing; Imager Pixel Spacing alone is not used (UI-24); measurements from pixel spacing alone say so (UI-23). | Tests on made-up files with each kind of spacing. | serious / rare |
| R-UI-04 | Measurement labels overlap and a number is read against the wrong line. | minor / likely | Labels move aside with a leader line (UI-25). | Test of label placement with crowded measurements; a capture check. | minor / rare |
| R-UI-05 | With patient details hidden, scans of two people in one export are taken to be one person's, and compared. | serious / rare | The rail says when an export holds more than one person and groups by person (UI-14). | Test with a made-up export of two people (`crates/voxa-app/src/ui/rail/whose_tests.rs::two_people_are_said_and_grouped`, `crates/voxa-app/src/ui/rail/whose_tests.rs::the_patients_name_is_nowhere_in_the_rail_while_details_are_off`), and of who is one person (`crates/voxa-app/src/people.rs::tests::studies_that_differ_in_identifier_or_name_are_two_peoples`). | serious / rare |
| R-UI-06 | Moving warnings out of the scans list hides that a series is only a plain stack (uneven spacing, mixed planes), so its 3D view or a measurement is taken as true. | serious / possible | A mark stays on the card; the full sentence is in the hover text and is posted on opening (UI-12); measurements on a plain stack say so. | Tests of the card mark and the notice (`crates/voxa-app/src/ui/rail/card_tests.rs::a_faulted_series_has_a_mark_and_no_warning_words_in_the_list`, `crates/voxa-app/src/ui/rail/open_tests.rs::opening_a_faulted_series_posts_its_sentence`); that measurements on a plain stack say so, `crates/voxa-app/src/measuring/tests.rs::a_plain_stack_says_so_in_the_list` and `crates/voxa-app/src/ui/tabs/measure/slice_tests.rs::a_plain_stack_says_so_above_its_measurements`; that slices of several sizes or planes are not measured at all, `crates/voxa-app/src/measuring/tests.rs::slices_of_several_sizes_or_planes_are_not_measured_and_say_why`. | serious / rare |
| R-UI-07 | A search result runs an action the person did not mean, and measurements or labels are lost. | minor / possible | Actions that throw work away ask first or can be undone (UI-07). | Tests of each such action through search. | minor / rare |
| R-UI-08 | Wording, or the order of what is shown, makes model labels or a measurement read as a finding. | serious / possible | Who made labels and the model's check come first (UI-26); no diagnostic wording (UI-38). | A wording test over the list of actions and help topics; review of new text. | serious / rare |
| R-UI-09 | A light theme lowers the contrast of letters (L, R) or outlines over the pictures, and left and right are misread. | serious / rare | The pictures and what is drawn on them keep the dark look in both themes (UI-29). | Captures in both themes; contrast test. | serious / rare |
| R-UI-10 | A report shown as text loses or reorders part of it, and the person misreads what it says. | serious / possible | The text keeps the report's order; it is headed as not the reference (UI-34); nesting and length limits say when something was left out. | Tests on made-up reports, including deep and long ones. | serious / rare |

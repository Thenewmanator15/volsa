# System requirements

- 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
- A graphics device with DirectX 12 or Vulkan. If Volsa cannot open its window, it says why in a
  message and closes. On a computer with no graphics card, Volsa can draw with Windows' own
  software graphics, more slowly.
- To label a brain scan, which is optional: a model folder made from files Volsa does not come
  with, Microsoft's Visual C++ 2015-2022 x64 redistributable, and a good deal of memory.
  [[Labelling a brain scan]] and
  [docs/models.md](https://github.com/Thenewmanator15/volsa/blob/main/docs/models.md) say what
  and how much.

Volsa has been tried on Windows 11 with an NVIDIA discrete graphics card and with Intel
integrated graphics. Windows 10 and other graphics devices have not been tried. Linux and macOS
are not yet offered.

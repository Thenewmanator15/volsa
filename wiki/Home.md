# Volsa

Volsa is a desktop viewer for MRI and CT scan exports, and other DICOM files at a basic level,
for 64-bit Windows. It opens a folder, a zip or a `DICOMDIR`, lists the studies and series inside
and says what kind each is. It shows a series as a stack of slices, as three planes through any
point of it, and as a volume in 3D that can be turned, cut open and saved as a picture; lengths,
angles and regions can be measured on it and points marked. It can also show the parts of a
brain that have been labelled, by another program or by a model Volsa runs on your own computer.
It makes no network connections.

**Volsa is not a medical device and is not for diagnostic use.**

## What Volsa is for

Volsa is for looking at MRI and CT scans, and other DICOM files at a basic level, on your own
computer: your own scans, and scans used for learning, teaching or research. It shows the
images, lets you measure them and can show labels made by a model, but it does not diagnose,
screen for or rule out anything, and its measurements and labels are not clinical results.
Questions about anyone's health belong with a qualified clinician. Volsa is not a medical device
and is not for diagnostic use.

What it is not for is on the
[intended-use page](https://github.com/Thenewmanator15/volsa/blob/main/docs/intended-use.md).
